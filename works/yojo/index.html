The projectYŌJŌ

We explored YŌJŌ before moving on to Chaska. The work remains a useful record of an idea taken far enough to design and test its story.

A brand for an everyday cup.

YŌJŌ started as a matcha-focused drinks idea. We explored what a more approachable brand could look and sound like, along with flavour directions and a website to bring the idea together.

Our team later changed direction for the venture we were building at Mesa. That was a decision about our own constraints and audience, not a claim that matcha cannot work in India.

The linked site presents the visual and product concept as an archive. It does not take orders or enquiries.
