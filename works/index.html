The projectEducational platform

OM Notes brings educational material for Office Management into one place, including OM-1 and OM-2 study resources and past papers, with a reader designed for students on mobile and desktop.

From landing page to live checkout.

I built the landing page, Google sign-in, Razorpay checkout, and account experience. Supabase handles authentication, payment-related functions, and access checks; Cloudflare Pages hosts the site.

A central challenge was making a long set of notes usable on phones while keeping the original document in private storage. The reader loads prepared pages as they are needed, with navigation and zoom controls.

01

A protected reader

The current reader displays prepared page images and overlays account information as a deterrent to casual sharing. Authorized viewers can still save or capture what they see, so the design does not promise to prevent copying.

02

Private source files

The original PDF stays in private storage. Authenticated requests retrieve individual prepared pages through an Edge Function, rather than sending the original PDF or a storage signing key to the browser.

03

Edge Function security pattern

Supabase Edge Functions handle operations such as checking entitlement and processing payment events. A page request checks the signed-in account and paid access before returning content; the service role key stays server-side.

04

A reader for long notes

The current prepared notes contain 164 pages. The reader loads pages around the viewport on demand, with page navigation and zoom for desktop and mobile.

The approach

Keep privileged operations server-side, check access on each page request, and make a long document practical to read on a phone.
