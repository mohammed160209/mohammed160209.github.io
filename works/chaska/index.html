The projectChaska

For Chaska, the website had to do more than look like a brand. It had to help people discover and buy the product.

From domain to checkout.

I built the Chaska website from domain setup through Shopify payment integration with help from one teammate. The work includes the surrounding setup a young brand needs to be visible and measurable online.

I also work across AI tools, data, Meta Ads Manager and pixels, and the operating systems behind the team’s execution. Those pieces keep changing as we learn what the business actually needs.

This is ongoing work. I’ll add verified outcomes and a closer look at the implementation as the venture develops.
